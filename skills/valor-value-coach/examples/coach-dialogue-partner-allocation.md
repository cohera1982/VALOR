# Example — Value Coach, draft-first flow (dynamic partner allocation)

**User:** We're building dynamic partner allocation. It matches idle drivers to vehicles that only have one driver, pairs two idle drivers onto a spare vehicle, or gives an idle driver a vehicle. Drivers can accept or reject.

**Coach:** Here's my first guess at the value:

> When **partner matching** lets **idle drivers get a vehicle or partner quickly**, **more vehicles work two shifts a day**, which **brings in more revenue from the fleet we already own**.

What's wrong or missing?
1. Mostly right
2. The real problem is something else
3. It's more about cost than revenue
4. I'm not sure who benefits

**User:** 1, and we've seen two-driver cars earn more.

**Coach:** That's the crucial link. It shows customers are there for the second shift. For matching to work, drivers have to say yes to the pairing. What makes a driver accept?
1. Same depot and a shift that suits them
2. Earning more
3. Getting on with the partner
4. Not sure yet

**User:** 1 and 3.

**Coach:** Good, that's exactly what will make or break it.

> When partner matching lets idle drivers get a vehicle or partner quickly **and they accept pairings that suit their shift and depot**, more vehicles work two shifts…

What would tell us it's backfiring?
1. Pairs split up after a few weeks
2. Driver complaints go up
3. Two-driver cars earn more but the fleet overall doesn't
4. Not sure

**User:** 1 and 3

**Coach:**

> When partner matching lets **idle drivers get a vehicle or partner quickly, and accept pairings that suit their shift and depot**, more vehicles work two shifts a day, which **brings in more revenue from the fleet we already own**.

| Value Story Card | Dynamic partner allocation |
|---|---|
| **Moves** | Share of vehicle shifts filled — *[epic outcome KPI to confirm — Value Owner]* |
| **Being used** | Share of pairing offers drivers accept |
| **Watch-out** | Pairs splitting within 30 days · Fleet revenue not rising overall |
| **Makes it happen** | Fleet Operations Manager |
| **Where it stands** | 🟡 Shaping → next: shifts filled today and revenue per two-driver car |

**Still needed**
1. Shifts filled today — *Fleet Operations*
2. Revenue per car before and after going two-driver — *Fleet data*
3. How drivers are paid (who gains from an extra shift) — *BU Finance*
