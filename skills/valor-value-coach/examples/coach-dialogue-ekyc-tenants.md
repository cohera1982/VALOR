# Example — Value Coach: revenue-led, third-party adoption, secondary obligation (e-KYC for tenants)

**User:** We're introducing e-KYC for property tenants.

**Coach:** I've read e-KYC as tenants verifying their identity online during onboarding. Here's my first guess:

> When **e-KYC lets prospective tenants verify their identity online**, **[who does what differently?]**, so leases are signed faster and fewer units sit empty between tenants, which brings in rent sooner.

What's wrong or missing? 1. Mostly right · 2. It's really about compliance and fraud · 3. It's about admin effort · 4. Not sure who benefits

**User:** 1

**Coach:** Good. Now the piece only you can fill: who has to do something differently? (pick any) 1. Tenants verify themselves · 2. Leasing team approves on the verified result · 3. Brokers send the link instead of collecting papers · 4. Not sure yet

**User:** 1, 2, 3

**Coach:** That's three groups changing at once, and two of them are outside our control, so whether they take it up is the first thing to watch. What happens today between a tenant saying yes and the lease being signed? (pick any) 1. Paper or email documents · 2. Back-and-forth delays · 3. Tenants drop out · 4. Not sure

**User:** 1, 2, 3

**Coach:** Tenants dropping out is the most valuable part: the paperwork is losing you tenants, not just time. How do we know they drop out because of the paperwork, rather than price? 1. We track drop-outs · 2. Tenants or brokers say so · 3. A hunch so far · 4. Not sure

**User:** 1

**Coach (one card, two quick questions):**
- Does this also help with any rules we must follow? 1. Yes, identity or anti-fraud checks are required · 2. It helps with tenancy registration · 3. No · 4. Not sure (Compliance to confirm)
- What would most tell us it's backfiring? Pick your top two. 1. Tenants abandon e-KYC · 2. Genuine tenants wrongly rejected · 3. Fraud gets through · 4. Vendor cost per check climbs

**User:** 4 (not sure) · 1 and 2

**Coach:** A tenant who walks away is usually replaced by another later, so the real gain is the empty days saved, not a year's rent. That keeps the claim believable.

> When e-KYC lets **prospective tenants verify online, with brokers sending the link and the leasing team approving on the verified result**, fewer applicants walk away and leases are signed sooner, which means **fewer empty days per unit and more rent collected**.
